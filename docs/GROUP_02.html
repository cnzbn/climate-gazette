How to read the E3CI

  • For every month, place and component (heat, cold, drought, rain, wind, hail, fire), the E3CI compares that month with the same month in 1981–2010: 0 = a normal month, 1 = one standard deviation above normal.
  • Values above 1 are extreme, in three classes: 1–2, 2–3, above 3 (the darker the colour, the more extreme).
  • Because every month is compared with itself, an extreme in winter or autumn means unusual for that season. For example, a heat E3CI of 1.2 in July and of 2.5 in January does not mean that January was hotter than July. It means that January was much further from what is usual for January than July was from what is usual for July.

Data: E3CI – European Extreme Events Climate Index, from the Copernicus ERA5 reanalysis.
